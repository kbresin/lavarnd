LavaRnd random walk

A walk on a grid. Every two random bits choose the next step: right, up, left or down. The path changes colour as it goes, from its start (the ring) to where it is now (the dot).

About this demo

After n steps a random walk is typically about √n steps from where it started: the root-mean-square distance is exactly √n. The walk wanders off, comes back near the start now and then, and doesn't prefer any direction. A biased generator would make it drift.

Each byte gives four steps. The walk stops after steps; start a new one to keep going.
